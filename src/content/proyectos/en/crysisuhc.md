Crysis UHC is an event, in collaboration with Crysis Studios, inspired by the famous game mode known as UHC Run, which consists in players having to quickly gather resources and equip themselves to fight. Where several people, alone and in teams, had to quickly equip themselves and fight to see who is the best across 3 matches.

This event was held as a closing event for the Crysis community, serving as one final event to bring the community together and have one last dance together.
